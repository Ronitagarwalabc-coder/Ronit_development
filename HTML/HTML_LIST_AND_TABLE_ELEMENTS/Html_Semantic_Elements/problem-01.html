Scholarships & Important Terminology

We are firmly committed to ensuring academic opportunities remain affordable for exceptionally gifted minds worldwide through robust institutional endowments and global financial aid networks.

Key Academic Terms to Know

Eligibility: The baseline benchmark metrics, academic requirements, and citizenship criteria an applicant must fulfill to be qualified for formal admissions consideration.Merit List: A competitive rank order breakdown of applicants calculated from performance scores used to automatically allocate limited-seat university programs.Scholarship: Financial assistance programs awarded to qualified students that do not require repayment, covering partial or complete tuition and living expenses.Application Fee: A non-refundable administrative processing fee required to submit and evaluate your formal application profile documents.
