Gla Admission Handbook

Admission overview -

Welcome to the official University Admission Handbook. Our institution is dedicated to fostering academic excellence, innovation, and diverse community engagement. This guide provides comprehensive information to assist prospective students in navigating the enrollment cycle for the upcoming academic year. Please note that the final application deadline is October 15, 2026, and late submissions will not be considered under any circumstances.

Eligibility -

To be considered for admission, applicants must satisfy both general university standards and specific program prerequisites It is a mandatory requirement that all applicants hold a recognized high school diploma or equivalent secondary education credentials with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale. Engineering and science streams strictly require advanced coursework completion in Mathematics and Physics prior to registration.

Application process -

Register an online account on the official university admissions portal.Complete the comprehensive digital application form with accurate personal and academic history.Upload clear, scanned copies of all necessary academic transcripts and identification. Pay the non-refundable processing fee through the secure online payment gateway.Submit the finalized application before the designated closing date and monitor your portal for updates.

Required documents -

Official high school transcripts or certified degree certificates.Proof of English language proficiency (such as TOEFL or IELTS scores for international students).Valid government-issued identification or passport copy.Two formal letters of academic recommendation.A well-crafted personal statement outlining academic and career goals

Important Terms -

Eligibility: The foundational academic and administrative criteria that an applicant must satisfy to be qualified for consideration.Merit List: The official ranked compilation of qualified applicants, generated based on academic performance and entrance examination scores, used to allocate available seats.Counselling: A structured interactive session where shortlisted candidates receive academic guidance and finalize their choice of stream or major based on merit ranking.Scholarship: Financial aid awards granted to outstanding students based on academic excellence, financial need, or athletic merit, which do not require repayment.Registration: The final administrative process where selected candidates officially enroll in classes, pay tuition fees, and secure their status as active university students.
